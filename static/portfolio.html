SymbolWeight

Weights are relative, not required to sum to 100 - e.g. 2 and 1 means the first ticker gets 2/3 of the amount. The amount is in the currency selected at the top (USD or EUR) and is saved with the portfolio. Assets priced in the other currency are converted at the live EUR/USD rate, so currency movement counts toward profit/loss. Switching the currency at the top only changes how existing portfolios are displayed.
