How to read this

Each asset's completed weekly returns are bucketed into Down, Flat and Up (Down/Up = more than half a standard deviation of that asset's own weekly moves, so a bond ETF and Bitcoin are each judged against their own normal range). A Markov chain then counts how often each state was followed by each other state over up to 10 years and applies that to the asset's current state.

Net Edge is how much better (or worse) the next-week Up-vs-Down odds are than the asset's usual odds, in percentage points. The label is No Clear Edge unless that edge is at least 5 points and statistically significant (~95%). Markets are close to memoryless, so most assets should read "No Clear Edge" most of the time — that is the honest result, not a failure. With ~150 assets scanned, a few will look significant purely by chance, so treat any "Favorable" or "Unfavorable" as a prompt to look closer, not a signal to act on. Also see the walk-forward backtest in each detail view.
